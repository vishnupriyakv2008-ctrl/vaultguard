How it works

  1. 1. Capture

    Photograph a receipt, product box, or warranty card.

  2. 2. Extract

    A vision LLM returns item, date, price, store and warranty length, stated or inferred.

  3. 3. Store

    Saved on your device. No backend, no account data in the cloud.

  4. 4. Track

    See days remaining and the dollar value of warranties still active.

  5. 5. Alert

    A local notification arrives a few days before expiry.

  6. 6. Act

    "Draft return email" writes a ready-to-send claim to copy or share.
